- **Scope:** one area, e.g. Peacock live-event infrastructure **or** one Comcast Business managed service
- **Targets agreed up front:**
  - Fewer alerts (noise reduction)
  - Lower MTTR
  - More tickets closed automatically
- **Bring references:** telecom and media customers using OpsRamp and Mist
- **Expansion path:** success in one area leads to the next entry point
