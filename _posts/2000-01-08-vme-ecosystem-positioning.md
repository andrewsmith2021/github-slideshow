title: "Ecosystem Gaps & Positioning"
---

**Often bigger than the feature gaps:**

- **Vendor certification:** many VNFs and appliances are certified only on ESXi, a likely Comcast blocker
- **Tooling:** confirm backup (Cohesity, Rubrik, Veeam), security, and monitoring support for KVM
- **Scripts & integrations:** PowerCLI, vCenter plugins, ServiceNow/CMDB need rework
- **Skills:** train operations staff on KVM and Morpheus

**Positioning:** move simple VMs first; keep NSX, vSAN, FT, and ESXi-certified workloads on VMware, managed side by side in Morpheus; fill gaps with Alletra, Zerto, OpsRamp, and Juniper; price replacements into the TCO.
