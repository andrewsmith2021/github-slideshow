title: "HPE vs. Dell for VMware Migration"
---

| | **HPE** | **Dell** |
|---|---|---|
| **Hypervisor** | Its own: **VM Essentials**, licensed per socket | Third-party (VMware, Nutanix, Red Hat, Microsoft) |
| **Management** | **Morpheus** runs VMware and HPE side by side | Mostly partner tools |
| **Migration & recovery** | Morpheus migration tools + **Zerto** | Mostly partner tools |
| **Observability** | **OpsRamp** before, during, and after the move | Mostly partner tools |
| **Compute** | **Gen13** 6th Gen AMD EPYC, iLO 8 security | PowerEdge (incl. 1P R6715/R7715) |
| **Support & buying** | One vendor; GreenLake or purchase | Several vendors; APEX or purchase |

<small>Per-socket licensing + high-core servers = fewer licenses, less power. Broadcom licenses per core, so denser servers raise the VMware bill.</small>
