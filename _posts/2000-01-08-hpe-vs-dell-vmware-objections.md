title: "Answering Dell's Objections"
---

- **"Choice of hypervisors, no lock-in."** Morpheus also manages other vendors' platforms, and HPE adds its own lower-cost hypervisor
- **"VM Essentials is less mature."** Agree; propose a phased pilot on ordinary workloads, keep VMware where needed, prove it with OpsRamp data
- **"We're the incumbent at the edge, cheaper at scale."** Compete on total cost: servers + licensing + power + operations
- **"Nutanix is proven."** It's another software vendor with its own pricing; HPE's per-socket model coexists with VMware

> "HPE delivers the whole path off VMware, under one contract, with one partner accountable for the result."
