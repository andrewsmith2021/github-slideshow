# Likely Interview Questions & Answer Frameworks

## Background / fit
1. Walk me through your background and why this role, at this time.
2. Why HPE? Why telco/cable specifically?
3. What do you know about HPE's current strategy in the telco/service-provider space? *(This is your chance to bring up Juniper + the HCLTech divestiture from company-and-role.md — shows real homework, not generic prep.)*
4. What's your experience managing Tier 1 / strategic enterprise accounts?

## Sales competency (map to story-bank.md)
5. Tell me about the largest deal you've closed. Walk me through the sales cycle.
6. Tell me about a time you built a relationship with a C-level executive who was initially skeptical or unavailable.
7. Describe a time you led a cross-functional or virtual team (SEs, partners, delivery) to close a deal. How did you keep them aligned without formal authority?
8. Tell me about a deal you lost. What happened, and what would you do differently?
9. How do you manage forecast accuracy? Walk me through your process from pipeline to commit.
10. How do you prioritize your time across multiple accounts or opportunities?
11. Describe a time you had to say no to a customer, or push back internally, to protect deal integrity or margin.
12. Give an example of identifying whitespace/new business in an account others considered "mature" or fully penetrated.

## Comcast-specific
13. What do you know about Comcast's business today, and where do you see the opportunity for HPE? *(Answer from comcast-account.md — lead with the AI Grid trial, then Comcast Business's strategic shift.)*
14. If you were taking over the Comcast relationship tomorrow, what would you want to know first?
15. Comcast is already running field trials of HPE AI Grid (HPE ProLiant + HPE Juniper + NVIDIA) — how would you think about turning a trial into a repeatable, expanding commercial relationship?

## Domain / industry acumen
18. How do you stay current on IT industry trends? What are you tracking right now in AI infrastructure or networking?
19. If you were selling into a Tier 1 telco/cable account today, what would you lead with — compute, networking, AI, or GreenLake — and why?
20. Who do you see as HPE's toughest competitor in this space, and how would you position against them? *(Have a real point of view — see competitive landscape in company-and-role.md.)*
21. How do you see AI changing what telcos/cable operators are investing in over the next 2-3 years?

## Situational / hypothetical
22. You inherit an account where the relationship is transactional and price-driven, with little executive access. What's your 90-day plan? *(Link to 90-day-plan.md.)*
23. A key deal is slipping and your forecast commit is at risk this quarter. What do you do?
24. Your customer's technical champion likes a competitor's solution better on paper. How do you handle it?
25. How would you build a business case to get incremental budget from a telco/cable CFO or CTO who's under cost pressure?

## Questions specific to reporting structure
26. How do you like to be managed / what do you need from a manager to be successful? *(Relevant since you'd report to Imran and work closely with Bob — show you understand team dynamics.)*
27. How do you handle disagreement with a peer account manager (e.g., Bob) on account strategy?

## Answer framework reminder
For behavioral questions: STAR, ~90 seconds, end with the *result* in numbers where possible, then a one-line "what I'd apply here." For strategy/hypothetical questions: state your framework first (e.g., "I'd start with account mapping, then...") before diving into specifics — shows structured thinking, which maps to the JD's "Strategic Planning" and "Business Acumen" competencies.
