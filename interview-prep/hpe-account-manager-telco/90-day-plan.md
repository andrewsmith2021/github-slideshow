# Draft 90-Day Plan (talking points for "how would you approach this account")

Use this as a structure to speak from if asked "how would you approach the account in your first 90 days" — don't read it verbatim, internalize the shape.

## Days 1-30: Learn & map
- Get fluent in HPE's current telco/SP portfolio and messaging post-Juniper/post-divestiture (compute, storage, networking, GreenLake, AI infrastructure) — see company-and-role.md.
- Shadow Bob Parsons and existing account calls; absorb account history, open opportunities, and relationship map.
- Build/validate a stakeholder map for the account: economic buyer, technical champion, procurement, incumbent vendors, known detractors.
- Review the account's public signals: recent earnings calls, capex commentary, network/AI modernization announcements, press releases.
- Identify what's currently being bought from HPE vs. competitors (Dell, Cisco, hyperscalers) and why.

## Days 31-60: Validate & plan
- Confirm/refine an account plan: whitespace opportunities, competitive risk areas, near-term renewal/expansion dates.
- Start building or deepening 1-2 executive relationships beyond the existing point of contact.
- Align with Imran and the extended team (SEs, specialists, partners) on priority plays for the next 2 quarters.
- Identify 1-2 "quick win" opportunities to build credibility fast, and 1-2 longer-cycle strategic plays.

## Days 61-90: Execute & show early results
- Have at least one active, qualified new opportunity beyond what was inherited.
- Deliver a concise account plan readout to leadership (Imran) with clear next-12-months goals.
- Establish a forecasting cadence/rhythm that gives early, honest visibility rather than surprises late in the quarter.

## Framing to use out loud
"I'd spend the first month listening and mapping before proposing anything — both the account's business priorities and how HPE's post-Juniper portfolio actually maps to them — then move fast on 1-2 concrete opportunities in months two and three so there's visible progress by the 90-day mark, not just a plan on paper."
