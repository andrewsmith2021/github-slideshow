# Questions to Ask Them

Pick 4-6 max per conversation. Lead with the ones tied to real research (marked ★) — they land best because they show you did homework specific to HPE right now, not generic interview-prep questions.

## For Bob Parsons (peer / current account team)
- ★ "Now that the Telco Solutions software business has moved to HCLTech and Juniper is fully integrated, how has that changed the day-to-day sales motion on this account — are you leading more with networking/AI infrastructure conversations than a year ago?"
- "How is the account team structured today — would I be co-owning the same account with you, or taking a distinct sub-account/product surface?"
- "What's the biggest unresolved challenge on this account right now that you'd want a new team member to move on quickly?"
- "What does great cross-functional collaboration with the SE/specialist teams look like here, in practice?"
- "What surprised you most about selling into this account (or this vertical) that you wish you'd known going in?"

## For Imran Jafri (VP, hiring manager / skip-level)
- ★ "Given your own background at Cisco and Motorola, how do you think HPE — post-Juniper — is genuinely differentiated in service-provider networking versus where Cisco sits today?"
- ★ "With the Telco Solutions divestiture to HCLTech, is HCLTech becoming a co-sell/referral partner in accounts that still need that OSS/BSS layer, or is that relationship fully separate now?"
- "What does success look like for this role at 90 days, and at the end of year one?"
- "How is the North America Telco/SP segment's growth strategy evolving — where do you want new headcount focused: net-new logos, expansion in existing Tier 1s, or both?"
- "What's the biggest gap on the team today that this hire is meant to close?"
- "How do you like account managers to handle forecast risk with you — early flags, or do you prefer confidence by the time it reaches you?"

## Generic but useful closers (either interviewer)
- "What would make someone unsuccessful in this role in the first six months?"
- "What are the next steps, and is there anything in my background you'd like more detail on before then?"

## Do NOT ask
- Anything answerable from the HPE newsroom/website (shows you didn't prepare) — e.g., "What does HPE do?"
- Compensation/benefits in an early-stage conversation unless they raise it first.
